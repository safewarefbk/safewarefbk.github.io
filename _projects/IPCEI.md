goals: >
 This project directly addresses the strategic EU objectives promoted in the Green Deal and European Cybersecurity 
  strategies by innovating the Multi-Provider Cloud-Edge Continuum in two primary directions.
  First, it focuses on decentralized and resource-efficient Edge AI, deploying intelligent capabilities directly to the 
  network edge to maximize scalability and efficiency via collaborative, distributed AI algorithms. These solutions 
  systematically optimize data workflows, power consumption, and processing loads while seamlessly adapting to diverse 
  hardware capabilities and computing environments.  
  Second, the initiative prioritizes trustworthy infrastructure through the development of 
  advanced security, privacy-preserving, and trust-aware frameworks. These mechanisms aim to reach robust operational 
  resilience and compliance with European regulatory standards and legal frameworks for decentralized AI-driven 
  applications.
